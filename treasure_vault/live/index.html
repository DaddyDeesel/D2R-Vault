Select Mules Shared stash only

Account shared stashes are always included. Choose characters below to include both their personal stash tabs and carried inventory. Unchecking a character removes both from the sale scope. Equipped and mercenary items are excluded.

Inventory Log

Off by default: track items in account shared stashes, materials tabs, and every character’s personal stash. Items picked up or dropped in a character’s backpack are ignored. Moving an item from a stash into a backpack appears as Removed from the stash; putting it back appears as Added.

Turn this on to also track carried inventory, including pickups, drops and moves between a stash and a backpack. Equipped items and mercenary gear are always excluded. This setting only affects the log; Select Mules controls what you can list for sale.
